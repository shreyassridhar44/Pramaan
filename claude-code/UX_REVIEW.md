# UX_REVIEW.md

Run this review at the end of Steps 4, 6, 8 and 10. Record findings with date, screen, severity (blocker, major, minor) and resolution.

## Checklist

### First impression
- Does the first screen after sign-in answer "how much do we hold, how much is proven, what needs action" within 5 seconds?
- Is there exactly one visual highlight per screen?

### Evidence clarity
- Can a user tell, for any value, where it came from, in one glance or one hover?
- Are conflicts impossible to miss, and is the conflicting character highlighted?
- Is the assurance level explained on hover with its evidence?

### Workflow efficiency
- Can a scrutiny officer triage ten queue items with the keyboard only?
- Does every action give immediate feedback and an undo or a clear confirmation where destructive?
- Are side sheets used instead of page jumps where context matters?

### States
- Skeletons match final layouts; empty states explain and offer the next action; errors show cause, retry and request ID.

### Language
- Plain, specific labels; sentence case; Indian formats; no jargon without explanation; no emoji or em dashes.

### Accessibility
- Focus visible and logical; charts have table views; motion respects reduced-motion; contrast passes.

### Responsiveness
- Officer app at 1280 and 1440; portal and verify page at 360.
